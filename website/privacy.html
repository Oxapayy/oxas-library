Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

This policy explains what data is processed when you use the oxapay.cc website, the web app at oxapay.cc/web, the Oxa’s Library apps and the optional Oxa account (together, “Oxa”). In short: Oxa has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. An account is optional.

1. Who is responsible

Oxa’s Library is an open-source project. The project’s maintainer is responsible for the data processing described here. You can reach us by opening an issue on GitHub.

2. Visiting the website

When you open oxapay.cc, our hosting provider Vercel Inc. and our DNS and security provider Cloudflare, Inc. process technical data that every website request contains: your IP address, the page requested, the date and time, and your browser’s user agent. This is needed to deliver the site and to protect it from abuse. We don’t use this data to identify you or build profiles.

3. Cookies and storage on your device

Oxa doesn’t use tracking, advertising or analytics cookies. It only stores what it needs to work:

  • Your cookie choice is saved in your browser so the notice doesn’t appear again.
  • The web app keeps your library, watch progress, watchlist, source connections and sign-in session in your browser’s storage, so the app works when you come back. Clearing your website data removes it.
  • The apps store the same information on your device.

Because this storage is strictly necessary, choosing Decline or Accept doesn’t change how Oxa works; no optional cookies are set either way. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie Settings at the bottom of each page.

4. Your optional account

If you create an account, we process your email address and password, and the library data you choose to sync: watchlist, watch progress, history and source connections. Source connections are stored encrypted with a key derived from your password. Passwords are stored by our account provider in hashed form, never in plain text. The account service is provided by Supabase, Inc., which hosts the account database on our behalf.

We use this data only to sign you in and to sync your library between your devices. You can delete your account and its synced data at any time in Settings › Account › Delete Account.

5. Using the web app with your sources

Browsers can’t read most media catalogues directly. To show a catalogue, the web app sends the source address and credentials you entered to our server function, which requests the catalogue from that source and passes it back. This data is used only for the request and isn’t stored by us.

  • Video loads directly from the playback server, or opens on the server’s own website. Those servers see your IP address, and their own privacy policies apply.
  • Artwork from The Movie Database loads directly from image.tmdb.org, which sees your IP address.

6. Downloads

App downloads are served by GitHub, Inc. When you download a release, GitHub processes your request under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

7. Donations

Donations are made with Bitcoin or Litecoin. Transactions are recorded on public blockchains. We don’t link them to accounts or any other data about you.

8. Legal bases

Where the EU or UK GDPR applies, we process data to provide the service you request (Art. 6(1)(b)), and to keep Oxa secure and working (legitimate interests, Art. 6(1)(f)). Where Swiss data protection law applies, we process data in line with the Federal Act on Data Protection.

9. Where data is processed

Vercel, Cloudflare, Supabase and GitHub may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, these transfers rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

10. How long data is kept

Technical request data is kept by our providers only as long as they need it for delivery and security. Account data is kept until you delete your account. Data stored on your device stays there until you remove it.

11. Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data, ask us to restrict or stop processing it, and ask for a copy of it. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. To use these rights, contact us through GitHub as described above.

12. Children

Oxa isn’t directed at children under 16, and we don’t knowingly process their data.

13. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
